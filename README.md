# Mint-Classics-SQL-Inventory-Analysis
SQL based inventory and warehouse analysis for Mint Classics Company using MySQL.

Recommendation: Mint Classics should consider consolidating the South warehouse into the West warehouse. West currently operates at approximately 50% capacity, providing substantially more spare capacity than the other warehouses, while South operates at approximately 75%. Based on the inventory levels analyzed, transferring South's 79,380 units to West would result in an estimated West utilization of approximately 81.8%, leaving approximately 18.2% spare capacity. This makes West the most suitable warehouse to absorb South's inventory based on the available data.

At the same time, Mint Classics should reduce excess inventory selectively rather than across the entire product portfolio. The 1985 Toyota Supra is a particularly strong candidate for review, with 7,733 units in stock and no recorded sales. Products with high stock-to-sales ratios and high inventory values should also be reviewed for purchasing reductions, promotions, or potential discontinuation. Conversely, products with strong sales and relatively low inventory should be protected from inventory cuts and may require replenishment.

The warehouse recommendation should be treated as an inventory-capacity recommendation rather than a definitive operational decision because the available database does not provide sufficient information about physical storage constraints, product-specific handling requirements, transportation costs, customer locations, or actual shipping times.
